A Life Well Lived

Arthur Edward Mitchell was born in Chicago, Illinois in 1938. Known for his steadfast character, warm sense of humor, and gentle wisdom, Arthur dedicated over 40 years as an engineer, craftsman, and community volunteer.

Beyond his professional achievements, his greatest joy was his family. He spent countless weekends teaching his children and grandchildren woodworking, gardening, and the art of patience.
