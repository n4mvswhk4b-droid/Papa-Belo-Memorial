A Life Well Lived

Isabelo Tagalogon Tapiculin was born in Himamaylan City, Negros Occidental in 1935. Throughout his life, he was known as a steadfast, hardworking father and devoted family man whose deep love and dedication provided a strong foundation for generations to come.

Life Milestones

  • 1935 – Born in Himamaylan, Negros Occidental, welcomed as the eldest son of Solpicio Tapiculin and Aquilina Tapiculin.
  • 1960 – Married Consolacion Trabajada, beginning a beautiful life journey together.
  • 1962 – Became a Father with the birth of his eldest son, Danilo Tapiculin.
